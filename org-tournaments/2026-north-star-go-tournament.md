# North Star Go Tournament 2026

## Rules

- Swiss system, 5 rounds
- 14 players
- all even games, 6.5 komi, Japanese rules (except: no draw from triple ko)

### Detailed rules

- Tie break: Wins > SOS > SOSOS
- Japanese rules
  - Japanese territory counting, no seki points
  - bent four is considered dead
  - except: no draw from triple ko
    - superko rule is applied, i.e. prohibit recurrences of an earlier board position

### Pairing

What do the numbers and + or - mean under each round? The number indicates who their opponent is and +/- is their result against that opponent.

For example:

- For player 2 in Round 1, "6+" means player 2 won against player 6
- For player 3 in Round 3, "5-" means player 3 lost against player 5

### FAQ:

- What pairing software was used?
  - We used MacMahon 3.10 by C. Gerlach. See: https://cgerlach.de/go/macmahon.html
- Why is there 1/2 in the SOS?
  - In the 4th round, two players were unable to play. They were not paired to avoid players to be matched with them and end up not playing with anyone. The MacMahon software automatically awarded unpaired players with a 1/2 score. In the final results, this does not affect the Wins which is the primary criteria for ranking, but the software includes it in the SOS which is used in tie-breakers. When a player is not matched due to an odd number of players, they get a BYE and wins by default. But for unpaired, it is not counted as a win, but granted 1/2 score. By default, MacMahon follows the EGF tournament system for players who do not play in a round. See https://www.eurogofed.org/egf/toursysrules.htm for this missed round exception.
